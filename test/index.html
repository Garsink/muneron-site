What can your team's AI
actually see and do?

Eleven questions about access, approvals and evidence. Review one person's setup, using settings and records you are already authorised to inspect.

This is a self-check, not a penetration test or a security certification. You may need your administrator's help. Finding the evidence can take longer than reading the questions.

Before you start

Do not attempt a real send, open a confidential file or change live permissions. Use existing settings, redacted records or the fictional examples provided. If you cannot inspect the evidence, choose Not sure.

Start by confirming the connected sources with the responsible person. Unknown access is not the same as no access.

How to mark an answer
  • Verified: you can inspect evidence that supports the criterion for this setup.
  • Gap found: the evidence shows a relevant control is missing or does not match the role.
  • Not sure: evidence is missing, inaccessible or inconclusive. An assistant's reassurance is not proof.
  • Not applicable: you have confirmed that the capability or workflow does not exist in this setup.
What stays in this browser?

Your selections and setup category are stored in this tab's browser session so a reload or a visit to the homepage does not normally erase them. Use Clear answers on a shared device. If your browser blocks session storage, we will tell you.

This page does not upload answers or company files and does not execute commands against your assistant. If you copy a suggested question to an AI service, that service's data rules apply. Use only non-sensitive information.
