# muneron.com

Static site for MUNERON, the company behind HAI. Deployed via GitHub Pages from `main` (CNAME `muneron.com`).

## Pages

- `/` — five-section introduction: clear promise, interactive cross-team example, three business benefits, real-company origin and first-workflow CTA. Essential access and AI-processing context stays visible.
- `/start-here/` — company deployment: discovery, source/access review, knowledge and rules, first workflow, validation and handover. Includes preparation, teaching, architecture, seven departmental use cases, data processing and practical questions. A jump navigation makes the longer guide scannable.
- `/aviation/` — the internal reference deployment: five-stage validation process and dated audit record, interactive verification example, operational timeline, functions, controls, channels and registry figures.
- `/start/` — company enquiry. Opens the visitor’s email application with company/team context, priority work, source outline, optional hardware preference and contact details. No form backend or analytics; the page does not send the message.

## Claim boundaries

No customer counts, ROI figures, certifications, prices or guaranteed installation timelines are claimed. Scale and audit figures are dated and describe the internal reference implementation. HAI Seed starts with approved information, analysis and preparation; connected actions and additional role environments are scoped and validated separately. Stage outputs are examples, with deliverables agreed in the proposal.

Teaching uses approved material, instructions and reviewed corrections. Shared knowledge does not imply identical access. Company memory is stored on the deployment machine; connected AI services process task information under the chosen accounts and service terms. Own-machine installation and a supplied preconfigured machine are both offered, with hardware and support terms agreed before delivery.

## Assets

Current pages use `assets/muneron.css` and the self-hosted Hanken Grotesk font. `assets/ui.js` controls the navigation disclosure at all viewport sizes, `assets/ask-panel.js` switches the homepage examples, and `assets/verify-demo.js` runs the reference audit demonstration. Logo direction 072 is in `assets/hai-072.png`. Existing illustrations, photographs, favicon and OpenGraph image are retained. Older asset files are retained for historical compatibility.

## Visual direction

The 21 October redesign takes its composition cues from microagi.ai: a full-height image-led opening, bold typography, an inset navigation bar, generous spacing, thin rules and open rows. Muneron retains its own identity, approved architecture illustration and company-wide product content. Seven department examples use native disclosures in the deployment guide. The menu remains in normal document flow without JavaScript and gains a compact disclosure when JavaScript is available.

Motion is limited to short transform-based control feedback, with hover effects limited to fine pointers and reduced-motion support. Evidence for the four-route responsive and interaction review is kept outside this repository at `outputs/muneron/microagi-redesign-2026-10-21/qa/`.
