For companies operating AI in day-to-day business, with jobs they cannot yet verify. A useful first message covers three points:

  1. Current AI operations. What the AI handles today, roughly how many jobs, and how often.
  2. Unverified jobs. The jobs whose output nobody has checked, or that report success without evidence.
  3. Exposure. The cost if one of those jobs is quietly wrong, if known.

Do not include customer files or confidential material. Replies come from the founder.
