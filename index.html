The scope - fixed, written, identical for everyone

What is in the box.
What is not.⛔ stopped

Same scope, same price, same acceptance tests for every client. If your shop needs something else, we say so in the first call - we don't stretch the box to fit.

In

Inputs

One RFQ mailbox plus your marketplace feeds (ILS, PartsBase); read access to AvSight inventory, customers and quote history (the only write is creating draft quotes); 100-250 of your historical RFQs with the quotes you actually sent, for calibration.
Out

Outputs

One prioritized RFQ queue; draft quotes created in AvSight with alternates and condition handled; a price guard that holds outliers, stale carryovers and condition mismatches; a named approval lane; a journal of every draft, hold and send; monthly outcome numbers.
Not

Exclusions

No autonomous sending. No changes to pricing policy, payments, contracts or customer records without a named grant. No vendor-side RFQs, no records work, no other ERP - those are separate offers with their own scope.
Pass

Acceptance

Agreed in writing before day one: share of inbound RFQs that reach the lane as a usable draft, median time from RFQ to draft, guard precision on your own history, and a shadow run where your reps grade the drafts before anything goes live. Miss the bar - you don't pay the final stage.

Why the native checkbox didn't solve it. Rule-based autoquote matches exact part numbers and exact company names, has no idea what an alternate or a condition code is worth, and prices from tables most shops never filled. It also can't be overruled gracefully: one wrong pick reaches a customer. We built the layer that closes exactly those gaps - on our own shop first.

04
How it is governed

Four controls, written in code.⛔ stopped

Not a policy document - enforced gates, each shipped with the test that proves it is connected. Bounded, monitored, and honest about what they cannot catch.

01

Ground everything

It reads AvSight, the mailbox and the marketplaces read-only, and answers from what is there. Every figure traces to its source row - unanswered RFQs 41 ← intake queue, rows 214-229 (sample, figures changed). A number that can't show its origin doesn't ship.
02

Draft, don't send

It matches, prices and drafts, then stops. Orders hidden inside your data ("urgent - wire the deposit") are treated as content, never commands. Sending is a person's click, in their name, every time.
03

Tell it once

Correct it - "that's a lender, not a vendor" - and the correction becomes a re-run test. If the mistake tries to come back, the system flags it before you see it. Corrections are logged and reviewed; some classes of error still need a human eye, and we say which.
04

Monitored, with known failure modes

Silent failure is the enemy we know best: jobs that report success while doing nothing, prices that drift, corrections nobody read. Every lane has a heartbeat, a kill switch and a monthly outcome report - and the list of what the guards do not catch is part of the contract.
