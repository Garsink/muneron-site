Operational AI

AI operations, verified.

MUNERON is founded on an AI operating layer built inside an aviation parts, MRO and landing gear leasing group. The layer reads across the business systems, drafts and routes work, verifies results against live sources, and keeps every outward action behind explicit controls.

This site documents what it does, what it is measured against, and what it got wrong.

What the layer does.

Nine functions inside the group. The person owning each process decides; the layer reads, drafts, checks and chases.

Enquiries and quotes

Read incoming requests, price them against stock and history, and draft quotes. Approved offers can send automatically under set rules, with manual-only control for any customer.

Purchasing

Track what needs buying, request supplier offers, and write the offers back into the system so nothing is asked twice.

Finance

Maintain a live view of cash, receivables and payables. Every figure is pulled fresh from its source, with bank data taking precedence over book records.

Market

Read market and regional sources each morning and report only the items that changed, with the source next to each.

Knowledge

Keep an append-only log of mail, chat, meetings and deals, separated into lanes so restricted material never reaches a shared summary.

Controls

Verify recipients, apply the lowest permission in any group, screen payment language, keep the ERP read-only by default, and audit the layer's own output.

Controls, built in.

Verification

No figure is shown unless it was pulled from the system of record at the time of writing.

Routing

Every recommendation reaches a named owner. Actions follow propose, approve, execute, confirm.

Outbound

Four outbound channels, each authorised, gated, logged and revocable. One master stop covers all four.

Fixed rules

The layer does not sign documents and does not move money. Reports to the owner are never gated, so a stop is always reported.

Audit correction.

One of the 20 manually reviewed findings was wrong.

The audit reported that a daily cash alert never arrived, after reading two lines in the log marked blocked. The next line, 308 milliseconds later in the same file, recorded the alert as sent. Across the full log, 108 of 132 alerts were sent.

The checker had treated an intermediate step as the final result. The finding was corrected, and the checker now reads the final delivery state before reporting on any send.

Built inside the business.

The layer was built by the group's CEO through written instructions and review, over four months: 1,650 commits across 15 repositories, without a developer typing code. It was built for one company, against that company's systems, and is not offered as a downloadable product. Founder's note ↗
