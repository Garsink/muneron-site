AI agents, built around your company.

Tell HAI what needs to get done. Working with you, it sets up a routine or an agent for the job: the company context it needs, the sources it may read, the rules it works under and when it runs. You define access and approvals. HAI supervises the work: what ran, what did not, and what needs a person.

  1. 1

    Define the job

    In plain words, from you or proposed by HAI from what it sees in the company.

  2. 2

    Configure around your company

    In a working session with you: company context, the sources it may read, the rules it follows, the tools it may use, the schedule.

  3. 3

    Run within approved access

    Only the sources and actions you granted, each with a stop switch.

  4. 4

    Supervise and surface

    What ran, what did not, what went quiet, and what needs a person, reported to the owner.

A familiar door

Your company has an email address.

Staff write to HAI the way they write to a colleague. The answer comes from the company's connected knowledge, decisions and current work, with sources and next steps, within the rules set for their role. Nothing new to learn, no new tool to open.

Illustrative example
From: a sales colleagueTo: HAI

Subject: Order 4471, where does it stand?

The customer is asking for an update on 4471. Where is it, and when can I tell them it ships?

From: HAITo: the sales colleague

Subject: Re: Order 4471, where does it stand?

Order 4471 is picked and packed. Collection is booked for Thursday morning. Tracking appears once the carrier scans the parcel; HAI checks for the confirmation and shares the update when it is available.

Source
ERP order record, updated 09:40 today · carrier booking confirmation
Owner
Logistics owns the collection. You own the customer update.
Next step
Tell the customer Thursday. HAI checks for the carrier confirmation and shares the available update.

Illustrative exchange. No real company, colleague, customer or order. In this illustrative company's configured view, supplier cost and internal margin are restricted for the sales role, so they do not appear in the reply. Who may ask, what each role may receive, and what never leaves the company are configured rules; every reply is logged. In the founding group, colleagues write to HAI by email and get answers from the company's context within their role. Part of a connected deployment.

Then it carries the work forward.

A customer asks for a quote on a part your purchasing team last bought eight months ago. Here is what happens between the request and the result.

  1. 1

    Source information

    The request, the ERP record, last year's supplier offer and the customer's open invoice are read together.

  2. 2

    The issue that matters

    The last supplier price is stale, and the customer has an overdue invoice. Both are surfaced, with the source next to each.

  3. 3

    Proposal with an owner

    A draft quote with a refreshed price check goes to the sales owner. The credit question is routed to finance.

  4. 4

    Decision remembered

    Finance approves on terms. The decision is recorded against the customer, with who decided and when.

  5. 5

    Follow-up with evidence

    Three days later: quote sent, acknowledged, order received. Or not, and the owner is told why, with the trail.

Questions owners ask.

Is this another chatbot?
No. A chat answers the question in front of it. HAI is a digital company: it holds the company context, connects approved sources, keeps decisions, runs agents and routines, and follows up. Talking to it is one of its doors.
Do we replace our systems?
No. Your ERP, accounting and mail stay the systems of record. HAI reads them and works alongside them.
Who controls access and actions?
You do. Access is granted per source and per role. Outbound actions exist only where you approve them, each with its own stop switch. HAI does not sign documents or move money.
How do we start?
With one workflow, its sources and its owner. Tell us about it, and we reply with what we would set up first and what we would need from you.
