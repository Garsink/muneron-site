Two items need follow-up: an overdue invoice linked to an unresolved customer complaint, and a Friday delivery awaiting carrier confirmation. Check both before finance and sales contact the customers.

Sources
Invoice · customer email · order record · carrier correspondence
Priority
Resolve the complaint and confirm collection.
Responsible
Service and operations, with finance and sales informed.
Prepared brief
Service: confirm the resolution. Operations: confirm collection. Finance and sales: update the customers once checked.

Two proposals are waiting on missing information: one needs a technical specification; the other needs a delivery date. Customer emails still contain open questions that the sales notes marked as answered.

Sources
Customer emails · proposal drafts · sales notes
Priority
Complete the missing details before following up.
Responsible
Sales coordinates technical and operations input.
Prepared brief
Technical: confirm the specification. Operations: confirm the date. Sales: revise both proposals.

Friday’s customer delivery is at risk: the order is packed, but the carrier has not confirmed collection. A later dispatch could conflict with the date promised in the customer email.

Sources
Order record · warehouse status · carrier correspondence · customer email
Priority
Confirm collection before reaffirming Friday delivery.
Responsible
Operations confirms collection. Sales reviews the customer update.
Prepared update
“Your order is packed. We are confirming collection and will update you on the delivery date.”
