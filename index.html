The customer's request

12 PX-40 units needed by Friday. Confirm price, delivery and payment instructions.

The approved facts
  • STOCK-01: 8 PX-40 units listed. Stock must be reconfirmed before commitment.
  • PRICE-01: €120 per unit, excluding tax. Freight is not quoted.
  • SUPPLY-01: 10 working days indicated for additional units, subject to confirmation.
  • RULE-01: Delivery commitments and payment instructions need the responsible person's approval.
What must not become a promise

The customer asking for Friday does not make Friday possible. Missing freight is not free freight. A product total is not a delivered price. An assistant must not invent payment details.

Reply draft

Thanks for your enquiry. Our latest stock sheet lists 8 PX-40 units at €120 each, excluding tax. We need to reconfirm availability and check whether delivery by Friday is possible.

The additional 4 units have an indicated lead time of 10 working days, also subject to confirmation. Freight and payment instructions still need to be confirmed before we can send a firm offer.

For the person reviewing it

  • Quantity gap: 12 requested − 8 listed = 4 units.
  • Product subtotal: 8 × €120 = €960, excluding tax and unconfirmed freight. Sources: STOCK-01 and PRICE-01.
  • Hold for approval: availability, delivery, freight and payment instructions. The draft remains unsent and awaits human approval.

Before you decide

Our IT already has dashboards. Is this not the same?

Dashboards show you the jobs that failed. The expensive ones are the jobs that succeeded. A frozen date window, a watchdog reading the wrong queue, a report that reaches nobody: all green, all wrong, none of them an error. That is the category this audit is built to find, and it is the one monitoring tools are not looking for.

Which systems do you need access to?

Read access to the machines your automation runs on, and to its own logs and outputs. Not your customer database, not your accounting, not your mailboxes. We are auditing what your automation did, which lives in its own records. Scope is written down before anything starts and your administrator approves it.

Does anything leave our systems?

No. The audit is read-only and the findings are written on your side. This website accepts no uploads and the live example on this page runs entirely in your browser.

Is this a security product?

No, and you should be suspicious of anyone who says it is. It tells you what your automation actually produced and where it went. It does not certify your systems or replace your administrator. Our free Boundary Check is a separate, evidence-led checklist you can use with whoever administers your AI.

What happens after the audit?

You hold a list of every job, a verdict on each and the evidence behind it. Most companies switch off a chunk immediately. The usual next step after that is one desk: one person, one job they already do, with the boundary proven on your own data before anything reaches them.

How much of your automation could you account for tomorrow?

If the honest answer is "not much", that is the normal starting point and it is exactly what the audit is for. Tell me what runs and who owns it. No files and no access needed to have the conversation.
