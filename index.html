Know where
your data goes.

The free check keeps your selections in this browser tab's session. It does not upload them, ask for your company files or run commands against your assistant.

If you use an AI provider to help interpret evidence, that provider's settings and terms determine what it receives. Use non-sensitive examples. Do not paste private records, passwords or client information into a test.

The pack will not make a cloud assistant local. Data flows and permissions need to be checked for the actual tools and connections on your desk.
