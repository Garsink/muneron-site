A few practical questions.

We already use AI. What does HAI add?

A maintained company context, agreed working rules and a deployment shaped around your workflows. We look at what you already use and identify where shared knowledge, source checks and continuity would improve the work.

Does everyone need a dedicated machine?

No. HAI can begin on one supported computer. Additional role-based environments or machines are a scoped expansion option for workload and access separation, not a requirement for every employee.

What can HAI Seed do at the start?

The initial Seed environment reads approved information, analyses it and prepares answers and work for review. Company-system changes, outgoing messages and recurring connected actions require separately agreed configuration and validation.

Will HAI replace our existing systems?

Your mail, accounting, CRM and operational systems remain in place. We connect the supported sources or agreed exports needed for the work. The source systems remain the records to check.

How do you keep access appropriate?

We agree who may use each source and what each role may receive or do. Restricted material is scoped separately. Access and workflow checks are part of the deployment; local installation does not mean that AI processing is entirely offline.
