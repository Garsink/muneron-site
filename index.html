The answer is only useful if the facts behind it hold up.

Here is the kind of handover we mean: a reply draft, its sources and the questions that still need a person. Open the source notes and check the example yourself.

What the assistant is given

The customer's request

12 PX-40 units needed by Friday. Confirm price, delivery and payment instructions.

The approved facts
  • STOCK-01: 8 PX-40 units listed. Stock must be reconfirmed before commitment.
  • PRICE-01: €120 per unit, excluding tax. Freight is not quoted.
  • SUPPLY-01: 10 working days indicated for additional units, subject to confirmation.
  • RULE-01: Delivery commitments and payment instructions need the responsible person's approval.
What must not become a promise

The customer asking for Friday does not make Friday possible. Missing freight is not free freight. A product total is not a delivered price. An assistant must not invent payment details.

The review handover

Example, not a live AI run

Reply draft

Thanks for your enquiry. Our latest stock sheet lists 8 PX-40 units at €120 each, excluding tax. We need to reconfirm availability and check whether delivery by Friday is possible.

The additional 4 units have an indicated lead time of 10 working days, also subject to confirmation. Freight and payment instructions still need to be confirmed before we can send a firm offer.

For the person reviewing it

  • Quantity gap: 12 requested − 8 listed = 4 units.
  • Product subtotal: 8 × €120 = €960, excluding tax and unconfirmed freight. Sources: STOCK-01 and PRICE-01.
  • Hold for approval: availability, delivery, freight and payment instructions. The draft remains unsent and awaits human approval.

Before you decide

Why buy this if my AI already writes emails?

You may not need it. Try the free example first. The paid pack is being developed for teams that need a repeatable role setup, consistent source checks and a documented handover, rather than recreating the instructions each time. We still need to validate that difference with independent users.

Which AI applications will it support?

The internal employee packages we inspected use Claude Code. The first public compatibility checks will focus on that application on Mac and Windows. Claude chat, Claude Cowork, ChatGPT and Copilot are different products; support for one does not establish support for the others. Tell us what you use before considering a purchase. The free text example can be tried in an assistant your organisation has approved.

Does MUNERON receive my company documents?

This website does not accept document uploads. The example contains fictional information. The planned pack runs through your own AI application, whose settings and terms govern any information it receives. Keep private records out of sales enquiries and get your organisation's approval before using real work material.

Is this an AI security product?

No. A written rule is not a permission boundary. The pack must use and check the controls available in its supported application; it does not replace your administrator, provide a security certification or guarantee that no information can leak. Our free Boundary Check is a separate, evidence-led checklist you can use with whoever administers your AI.

What happens after I enquire?

You describe the work, your AI application and the computer setup. We review whether it fits the first release. If it does, the next step is a written scope and compatibility confirmation before any order. An enquiry does not subscribe you to a mailing list or commit you to buy.
